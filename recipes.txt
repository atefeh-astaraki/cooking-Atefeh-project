First recipe:
Fesenjan
Ingredients:
- Chicken
- Walnuts
- Pomegranate molasses
- Onion
- Salt
- Pepper
Cooking method:
1. Chop the onion and cook it until soft.
2. Add the chicken and cook it for a few minutes.
3. Add the ground walnuts and some water.
4. Add the pomegranate molasses.
5. Let it simmer slowly until the sauce becomes thick.
6. Add salt and pepper to taste.
